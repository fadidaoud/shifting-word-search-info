# Privacy Policy — Shifting Word Search

Effective November 11, 2026

Shifting Word Search is an offline word puzzle published by Fadi Daoud. You can play without creating an account or connecting to the internet. The app stores completed-board counts and daily streaks on your device using local storage. We do not operate an account service or receive your puzzle progress.

## Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video hints and may show an interstitial after you choose the next board following every third completed board. Ads require an internet connection. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent platform. Google may collect or process IP address, approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement. The data and uses depend on your location, device settings, and consent choices. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures for [iOS](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure) and [Android](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Where required, the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit available choices through the shield icon labeled Privacy choices in the app. If consent is unavailable or an ad cannot load, the puzzles remain playable. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally access the iOS advertising identifier for personalized tracking. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may read the Android advertising ID for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset it or delete it in your device settings under Privacy > Ads.

## Data retention and control

Puzzle progress remains on your device until you erase the app's data or uninstall the app. We cannot recover or delete this local progress remotely. Google's handling and retention of advertising data is governed by its policies and your consent choices. To change those choices, use Privacy choices in the app when available or your device privacy settings.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience. It does not ask for names, messages, or contact details. If a child uses the app, a parent or guardian should review ad and privacy settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the [support page](support.html). Do not include personal information in a public issue.

We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
