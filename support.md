# Support — Shifting Word Search

For gameplay or technical help, [open a support issue](https://github.com/fadidaoud/shifting-word-search-info/issues/new). The app is available for iPhone and Android. Describe your device model, its iOS or Android version, the screen where the issue happened, and steps to reproduce it. Do not include passwords, ad identifiers, or other private information in a public issue.

The puzzle works offline. Ads and rewarded videos need internet access and may be unavailable if consent or ad inventory is unavailable. The free hint remains available.
